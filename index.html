01 Work

Building things
people want to use.

I enjoy taking an idea into the real world: understanding what customers need, setting up a pilot, and learning from how people use it. That entrepreneurial approach runs through both my work within established organizations and the ventures I’ve built myself.

Piloting an interactive test drive

A project designed to help prospective customers explore a car’s features during the drive itself, with less need for a salesperson to explain each one. I contributed from the outset, focusing on pilot setup, day-to-day operations, and improvements to the experience.

Launching a travel-tech venture

I founded and launched a gamified tourism platform designed to make travel experiences more engaging and create new revenue opportunities. I took it from business concept to launch, including the website and app.

03 Life

German roots.
Brazilian ease.

I was born in Germany and hold German and Brazilian nationality. Both are part of who I am: I appreciate punctuality and doing things properly, alongside a relaxed outlook and a little Brazilian jeitinho when a situation calls for ingenuity.

That mix feels quite natural to me: I like having a plan, and I also like leaving some room to improvise. Traveling and moving between languages add to that perspective. There is usually more than one way to understand a situation—or find a way through it.

Family, my dog, and time away from a screen are part of the picture too.
